Terms and conditions

These terms explain the SwiftHaul SA account and delivery-booking service. Read them before creating an account. Contact dispatch if you need clarification.

1. Your account

Provide accurate contact details, keep your sign-in details private, and use the platform only for lawful activity. Customer, Driver and Company accounts are managed separately. Company operations require administrator access.

2. Delivery details

Supply complete pickup and drop-off addresses and accurate recipient information. Provide the actual weight and outer dimensions of the packed consignment. The platform recommends a vehicle from the chargeable weight; confirm physical cargo fit with dispatch. Do not include passwords or payment-card details in delivery notes. Contact dispatch about load size, restricted goods or special handling before booking.

3. Distance and pricing

For new bookings, the platform calculates driving distance from the mapped pickup and drop-off locations. Kilometres are calculated automatically; customers must check that the matched locations are correct. The base fare applies from 0 km to below 12 km. The fare doubles at each additional 12 km boundary, using the unrounded distance.

Editing either address, the actual weight or the packed dimensions requires a fresh quote. The booking stores the quoted fare and mapped distance you reviewed. Earlier bookings retain their original prices.

Chargeable weight and additional fees

All dimensions are in centimetres. Volumetric kilograms = length × width × height ÷ 5,000. Chargeable weight is the greater of actual and volumetric kilograms. For multiple parcels, enter the combined actual weight and the outer dimensions of the complete packed load.

Bike is selected up to 20 chargeable kg; its base distance fare includes 5 kg and additional weight costs R0.85/kg. Van is selected above 20 kg up to 1,000 kg; it includes 50 kg and additional weight costs R0.50/kg. A 4-ton Truck is selected above 1,000 kg up to 4,000 kg and an 8-ton Truck above 4,000 kg up to 8,000 kg. Both truck classes include 1,000 kg. Extra weight costs R0.045/kg for the 4-ton class and R0.05/kg for the 8-ton class. Above 8,000 kg requires a manual quote.

The weight fee is charged proportionally for fractional extra kilograms and rounded to cents, then added once to the distance-band fare. Distance-band doubling applies to the distance fee only. The quoted breakdown is saved with the booking. Physical cargo fit, legal loading and access require dispatch confirmation.

4. Map routes and vehicle suitability

Address search, route calculations and maps rely on third-party mapping services. Address matches, road data and estimated driving times may be incomplete or inaccurate. A route preview does not show a live driver position or live traffic, and does not verify truck height, weight or access restrictions. Confirm the route and vehicle suitability with dispatch where needed. If a quote or route cannot be calculated, contact dispatch rather than entering an estimated fare.

5. Booking, payment and changes

Submitting a booking records a delivery request; it does not confirm payment, driver availability or a guaranteed arrival time. WhatsApp opens a prepared message that you choose whether to send. Contact dispatch about availability, cancellations, changes, additional requirements or payment arrangements. Any revised amount should be agreed before the delivery proceeds. An invoice is not proof of payment; VAT details appear only when supplied by the business.

Cash payments and counteroffers

Cash payment may be requested only when the mapped outbound route is strictly below 12 km. At 12 km and above, arrange payment through dispatch. A cash request does not mark an invoice paid. Customers may submit a counteroffer only for routes strictly below 24 km. Offers remain pending until company approval; the original quoted fare remains in place until acceptance. Return deliveries require a separate quote. Confirm payment and any offer approval with dispatch.

Wallet, rewards and pay later

Refund money may pay for deliveries or be withdrawn. Promotional credits pay for deliveries only and cannot be withdrawn or transferred to another customer. The wallet spends promotional credits first. The platform credits 20% of each paid fare from the fourth paid, completed delivery onward in each calendar month, using South African time. Qualification occurs when both payment and delivery completion are confirmed.

A new customer may enter a referral code at sign-up. After their first paid, completed delivery, both accounts receive 15% of that fare as promotional wallet credit. Referral rewards are issued once. Refunding a delivery reverses its rewards; spent promotional rewards are offset against future promotional credits. Wallet payments and refunds are recorded in the activity ledger.

Pay later requires a verified account, at least three completed deliveries and no overdue balance. Each transaction and the combined outstanding balance may not exceed R500. Repayment is due seven days after borrowing. Repay through Paystack; there is no automatic debit. Overdue accounts cannot borrow.

Instant withdrawal requests carry a fee of 2.5% of the requested amount plus R5, with processing estimated within a few hours. Standard withdrawals carry an R2 fee and are submitted the next business day, with an estimated 1–2 business days for processing. Fees are deducted from the requested amount. Bank and provider availability affect timing. Requested funds remain reserved until payout confirmation; verified failed or reversed payouts return the reserved amount including its fee.

Online payments and withdrawals require the configured payment provider. Returning from checkout does not confirm payment. Provider verification determines payment and payout status. Drivers confirm assigned deliveries using the recipient PIN. Share the PIN only after receiving the delivery. Cash receipt is recorded by the assigned driver when eligible. Company administrators may approve counteroffers, assign drivers and refund a paid booking to the customer wallet. These actions are authenticated; customers cannot credit their own wallet or confirm their own payment.

6. Account and delivery information

Account information is handled through the account service. Booking addresses, recipient notes, fares and route details are stored to operate the delivery platform. Address and map requests are sent to mapping providers when calculating or viewing routes. Opening WhatsApp or Google Maps uses those services. Avoid adding information that is unnecessary for delivery, and contact dispatch about your stored booking details.

7. Acceptance and contact

The sign-up checkbox records agreement to this version of the terms. Account records include the accepted version and the acceptance time. Guest previews do not create an account or delivery booking. Questions about these terms or your delivery can be sent to SwiftHaul dispatch on +27 61 508 3880.

Scheduled pickups, returns and customer settings

Scheduled pickup and return times are requests subject to dispatch confirmation. Return delivery is quoted separately and is excluded from the outbound fare. Customer settings may contain private contact details, a profile picture and banking details supplied for service administration. Saving banking details does not authorize a debit or confirm payment. Wallet balances, repayments and pending payment or withdrawal transactions must be settled before profile deletion. Financial history is retained with a hashed account reference. Profile deletion removes the customer account and saved customer profile records; it does not cancel an active dispatch. Download invoices before deletion and contact dispatch about active deliveries.

8. Goods-in-transit protection and insurance enquiries

No customer goods-in-transit insurance is currently active through SwiftHaul. SwiftHaul has not selected or contracted an insurer for customer parcels, and no cover limit, premium, excess, insured events, exclusions, policy wording or claims process has been confirmed for this service. The previous proposed R50,000 summary is withdrawn and must not be treated as an insurance offer or evidence of cover.

Nedbank Insurance publicly describes goods-in-transit cover within its business insurance offering, including heavy-commercial-vehicle and marine insurance solutions. This does not confirm that Nedbank has agreed to insure SwiftHaul's last-mile deliveries or individual customer parcels. Any product would require direct underwriting confirmation, eligibility approval, final policy wording and a written agreement.

The optional Nedbank Insurance checkbox at checkout records only that you would like SwiftHaul to contact you about possible cover. It is an enquiry/interest indication only. It is not an insurance proposal, policy purchase, premium payment, acceptance of policy terms, or confirmation that any goods are insured. Selecting or not selecting it does not change the delivery booking, fare, or SwiftHaul's responsibilities under applicable law. No insurance premium is charged through checkout.

If a suitable policy is later agreed, SwiftHaul will publish the insurer's approved policy wording, cover period, insured value limits, premium, excess, exclusions, claim notification deadlines and evidence requirements before offering it. Cover will apply only after the insurer has accepted the risk and issued the required policy/certificate for the relevant consignment. Do not assume goods are insured while waiting for confirmation.

Customers should accurately describe their goods, declare their value to dispatch where requested, package items appropriately and report any loss or damage promptly. Any incident will be handled under the delivery terms and applicable law; this does not create insurance cover or limit statutory consumer rights.

9. Optional loading, unloading and in-home assistance

The standard delivery fare covers transport only and does not include carrying items through a property, up or down stairs, dismantling or assembling furniture, installation, packing/unpacking, or positioning items inside a room. If you need a driver or additional helper to assist with any of these tasks, tell SwiftHaul before dispatch so the company can confirm availability, staffing, safety and the additional fee.

Basic loading, unloading and carrying assistance may be requested at no additional charge. It is subject to vehicle suitability, driver availability, safe handling and dispatch confirmation. The number of helpers and time allowance depend on the vehicle and operational availability. Oversized or unusually heavy items, stairs, long carries, restricted access, special lifting equipment, dismantling/assembly and waiting time may require a separate quote. Drivers may refuse unsafe or undisclosed handling.

There is no separate fee for requesting basic help with loading, unloading or carrying items. Bike deliveries cannot carry a separate helper. Stairs, unusually heavy or oversized items, long carries, restricted access, special lifting equipment and dismantling or assembly must be discussed with dispatch in advance; any genuinely separate specialist service must be agreed with the customer in writing before it is provided. No unapproved assistance fee may be added to a booking.

Customers should identify fragile, heavy and high-value items and advise whether there are stairs, lifts, narrow passages, long walking distances or other access constraints. The driver must not undertake unsafe lifting or work beyond the agreed scope. The customer's request for assistance does not transfer responsibility for a driver's negligent handling to the customer.

10. Customer acknowledgement at booking

Before submitting a booking, the customer must tick the acknowledgement confirming that they have read and understood these terms. The acknowledgement records acceptance of the disclosed service scope and the fact that customer parcel insurance is not currently active through SwiftHaul. It is not a waiver of statutory consumer rights. The optional Nedbank enquiry checkbox is separate and does not create insurance cover.
