How your quote is calculated

Confirm both map pins and enter your parcel dimensions and weight in the customer workspace. SwiftHaul then calculates the route-band distance fee and any applicable chargeable-weight fee. The booking screen shows the live fare before you book.

Privacy overview

SwiftHaul processes information needed to provide delivery services, support account security, coordinate deliveries and maintain relevant business records. Personal information should be handled in accordance with applicable South African privacy law, including POPIA. For requests about your information, use the contact route provided inside the relevant SwiftHaul platform. This short overview is not a substitute for the full privacy policy.

Terms overview

SwiftHaul connects customers with delivery drivers and transport partners. Customers must provide accurate collection, delivery and goods information and may not submit prohibited or unlawful items. Delivery estimates are not confirmed bookings; final acceptance, availability, pricing and service conditions are shown in the booking and dispatch process. The full terms presented in the platform apply.
