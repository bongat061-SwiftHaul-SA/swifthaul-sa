Driver insurance administration

Manage insurer-issued policies for active Premium drivers. Cover is not active until the insurer has issued the policy and the backend confirms subscription eligibility and coverage dates.

Benefit schedule: R30,000 vehicle cover · R100,000 accidental disability · R1,000 per eligible accident-related day.

Sign in as an administrator to manage insurer policies.

Insurance claims review

Review assigned-dispatch evidence, GPS trip history, supporting documents and insurer decisions. Claims cannot be approved until route compliance is confirmed and the insurer has approved the claim.

Daily benefit is R1,000 for a maximum of 5 days / R5,000 per accident claim. Being online without an assigned dispatch does not qualify. Any unexplained stop or diversion must be recorded as a route-compliance breach.

Sign in as an administrator to review insurance claims.

04 / Driver payouts

Review driver bank payout requests. Approving a request reserves it; mark it paid only after the EFT has actually cleared and you have its bank reference.

Bank details are shown masked. Manual EFT only; no automated bank transfer is connected.

Driver cash reconciliation

Review driver deposit instructions and proof. Reconcile only after matching the exact amount and unique reference against the company bank statement. A driver-uploaded receipt or screenshot is not settlement confirmation.

Sign in as an administrator to review driver deposits.

Customer cash receipts

Signed cash receipts are audit evidence. Compare these receipts with deposits before clearing the driver's cash-held balance.
