Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SwiftHaul SA collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our customer, driver and company services. It is intended to explain our current service purposes in plain language and should be read with the Terms of Service.

1. Information we may process

Depending on how you use SwiftHaul, information may include your name, email address, telephone number, account and sign-in records, identity number and verification results, driver's licence details and expiry date, vehicle and registration details, profile photograph, pickup and drop-off addresses, Plus Codes, location coordinates and trip-location updates, recipient details, delivery instructions, booking and dispatch history, support messages, payment references, wallet and payout records, and consent preferences.

Do not submit information about another person unless you are authorised to provide it for the delivery. Only provide information needed for the service.

2. Why we use personal information

We process relevant information to create and secure accounts; verify customer and driver identities and driver eligibility; onboard drivers; match and dispatch deliveries; calculate routes and quotes; provide delivery tracking and proof of delivery; communicate about bookings, safety and account security; process payments, refunds and payouts; prevent fraud and misuse; respond to support requests; maintain service records; and meet applicable legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

3. Location information

Pickup and drop-off locations are used to plan and dispatch deliveries. Where a location feature is enabled and permission is granted, driver location information may be processed for trip dispatch, operational tracking and delivery safety. You can manage device location permissions through your device settings; disabling location access may limit location-dependent features.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared, where necessary for the purposes above, with the assigned driver, customer or recipient; authorised SwiftHaul administrators and dispatch personnel; identity or document verification providers; hosting, database and security providers; mapping and geocoding services; payment and payout providers; and communications providers. Service providers are expected to protect information and use it only for their contracted service purposes. We do not sell personal information.

5. Direct marketing

Promotional SMS, WhatsApp or email messages are sent only where you have separately opted in or where otherwise permitted by applicable law. The marketing checkbox is optional and is not required to create an account or use core delivery services. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time by using an unsubscribe instruction where provided or contacting SwiftHaul. Service, security, booking and legal notices are not promotional marketing.

6. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the service purposes described here, account administration, dispute handling, security and applicable legal or financial record-keeping requirements. Retention periods may differ by record type. When information is no longer required, we take steps to delete, de-identify or securely dispose of it, subject to lawful retention obligations.

We use technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password and six-digit PIN confidential and contact SwiftHaul promptly if you suspect account misuse.

7. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of personal information, ask questions about how it is used, object to or withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis, and request deletion where we are not legally required to retain the information. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully carried out and may mean we cannot provide features that require the information. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children

SwiftHaul accounts and driver onboarding are intended for adults. Do not create an account or provide a child's personal information unless this is necessary for a delivery and you are authorised to do so.

9. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, consent withdrawal or complaints, contact SwiftHaul through SwiftHaul support on WhatsApp. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa through its official website at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Updates

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The version shown above identifies this policy. Where a material change requires renewed consent, we will request it separately.

Last updated: 11 November 2026. This policy is general service information and is not legal advice.
