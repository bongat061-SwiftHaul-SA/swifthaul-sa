Insurance claims

Submit an accident claim only if it happened during an accepted dispatch: while travelling to the customer pickup address or travelling from pickup to delivery. You must be ONLINE, have active insurer cover and keep live GPS route evidence enabled. Being online without an assigned dispatch is not covered.

Daily accident benefit: R1,000 per day, maximum 5 days / R5,000 per accident claim. Unexplained stops or diversions from the authorised route may invalidate a claim. All claims are subject to insurer review.
